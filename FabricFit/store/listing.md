## Full description (≤ 4000)

```
Scan Your Fabric. Wear Your Style.

FabricFit turns the cloth you already own into your next outfit. Photograph kente, ankara,
wax print, lace or any fabric in your collection and FabricFit reads its colours, pattern
and texture — then shows you the styles it could become.

BUILT FOR GHANAIAN & AFRICAN FABRIC
FitScan reads the fabric you actually have: kente, adinkra, wax print, lace, brocade,
denim, batakari and more. Analysis runs on your phone, so you can scan at the market, at
home or in the shop with no connection.

FITMATCH AI
Every recommendation explains itself. FitMatch AI ranks designs for women and men by
occasion — traditional, wedding, church, office, party, everyday — and tells you why a cut
suits your cloth: how the motif scale reads, how the pattern moves, which colours flatter
it. No black box.

FITROOM
Add one photo and preview a design on a person before you spend a cedi at the seamstress.
FitRoom is honest about what it is: an on-device preview showing colour, scale and cut —
and a real generation backend when you connect one.

MY FIT CLOSET
Keep the pieces you own, build outfits from them, and save the looks you love. Your closet
travels with you, on your device.

MADE FOR TAILORS
Share a design brief — fabric, design, customisations and your own measurements — as a
clean PDF any seamstress or tailor can work from. FabricFit never contacts tailors or takes
payment on your behalf.

YOUR PHOTOS STAY YOURS
No account. No adverts. No ad SDK, no advertising identifier. Fabric photos are analysed on
your phone and never uploaded. An image only leaves your device when you ask for cloud
analysis or a FitRoom generation, and the app tells you every time it happens.

SPEAKS YOUR LANGUAGE
English, Twi, Ga and Ewe.

FabricFit — "ntoma" is Akan for cloth. Where heritage fabric meets modern fit.

Made in Accra, Ghana.
```
