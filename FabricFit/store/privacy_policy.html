FabricFit — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 22 November 2026

FabricFit ("the app") is designed around a simple principle: your photos and choices stay on your device. This policy explains, plainly, what the app does and does not collect.

What we collect

Nothing is transmitted by this build. The app contains no analytics SDK, no crash-reporting transmission, no advertising SDK and no advertising identifier. There is no account, no sign-in and no server-side profile. No data is sent anywhere unless you turn on a cloud feature, as described below.

The app uses the system Photo Picker to let you choose images, so it never needs broad storage access. It does not request contacts, location, microphone, SMS, phone, call-log, accessibility or Bluetooth permissions.

On-device AI, and the optional cloud engine

By default, fabric analysis and try-on previews run on your device with bundled models and are labelled honestly in the app (e.g. "on-device demo"). Nothing is uploaded.

The app also contains an optional cloud engine, which is off until you configure it. Settings → Analysis engine lets you paste the address of a backend you choose; until you do, the app shows "No cloud endpoint is configured" and stays on-device. When you do configure one and use it, the image you selected is uploaded to that address so the model there can process it, and the analysis screen says so at the time. We operate no server of our own and receive nothing; whoever runs the address you enter receives the image and is responsible for it, which is why the field is yours to fill in rather than one we pre-fill.

Photos are sent for that single request. No account, identifier or history is attached, and the app never uploads in the background. Removing the address, or switching back to the on-device engine, stops all transmission.

Your choices & deletion

Settings → Data controls → Delete my data removes the on-device database, media files and usage quotas in one tap. Uninstalling the app removes everything else, since nothing lives outside your device.

Children's privacy

The app is not directed to children under 13 and collects no personal information from anyone.

Changes

If a future version changes any of the above (for example, adding an optional cloud feature), this policy will be updated in-app and on the store listing before the change takes effect.

Contact

Anything about this policy, including a data-deletion request: fabricfit.app@gmail.com. The same address is shown in the app under Settings → Help, and it is the address on our Google Play listing, so there is one place to write rather than several.
