## Scanning Fabrics

### How to Scan

1. **Tap the Create button** (center of bottom nav)
2. **Select "Scan Fabric"**
3. **Grant camera permission** if prompted
4. **Position the fabric** in the camera viewfinder
5. **Tap the shutter button** to capture

### Best Practices for Scanning

✅ **DO:**
- Use good lighting (natural light is best)
- Lay fabric flat on a plain surface
- Fill the frame with the fabric pattern
- Hold the camera steady
- Capture from directly above

❌ **DON'T:**
- Scan wrinkled or folded fabric
- Use flash (creates glare)
- Scan in low light
- Include shadows in the frame
- Move the camera while capturing

### Understanding Scan Results

After scanning, you'll see:

**Fabric Analysis:**
- **Category** - Type of fabric (Kente, Ankara, etc.)
- **Pattern** - Design classification (Striped, Geometric, etc.)
- **Confidence** - How certain the AI is (0-100%)
- **Colors** - Dominant color palette
- **Texture** - Fabric texture analysis

**What You Can Do:**
- 💾 **Save** - Add to your fabric collection
- 🎨 **Design** - See outfit recommendations
- 📤 **Share** - Share with friends
- 🗑️ **Delete** - Remove the scan

## Virtual Try-On

### How It Works

Virtual Try-On lets you see how fabrics look as different outfit styles using AI technology.

### Creating a Try-On

1. **Select a fabric** (from scan or favorites)
2. **Tap "Try On"**
3. **Choose a style** from recommendations
4. **Upload your photo** or use a model
5. **Wait for AI processing** (10-30 seconds)
6. **View your virtual outfit!**

### Tips for Best Results

**Photo Guidelines:**
- Full body shot, standing straight
- Good lighting, plain background
- Wear fitted, neutral-colored clothing
- Face the camera directly
- Arms at your sides

**What to Expect:**
- AI generates a realistic preview
- Results vary based on photo quality
- Some styles work better than others
- It's a preview, not a perfect simulation

### Saving Try-On Results

1. **Tap "Save Look"** after generation
2. **Add a title** (optional)
3. **Choose to share** or keep private
4. **Find saved looks** in Favorites → Looks

## Building Your Wardrobe

### What is the Wardrobe?

Your Wardrobe is a digital catalog of all your fabrics and outfits, organized for easy access.

### Adding Items

**From Scans:**
- Scanned fabrics auto-save to Wardrobe
- Find them in Wardrobe → Fabrics

**From Try-Ons:**
- Saved looks appear in Wardrobe → Outfits
- Includes fabric and style details

### Organizing Your Wardrobe

**Fabrics Tab:**
- View all scanned fabrics
- Sort by date, category, or color
- Search by name or pattern

**Outfits Tab:**
- View all saved looks
- Filter by occasion or style
- See which fabrics were used

### Using Collections

**Create a Collection:**
1. Go to Profile → Collections
2. Tap "Create Collection"
3. Name it (e.g., "Wedding 2026")
4. Add fabrics and looks

**Benefits:**
- Organize by event or theme
- Quick access to related items
- Share entire collections

## Sharing & Exporting

### Share Fabrics

**From Fabric Details:**
1. Open any fabric
2. Tap the **share icon** (top right)
3. Choose platform:
   - Instagram
   - Twitter/X
   - Facebook
   - WhatsApp
   - More...
4. Add caption (pre-filled with details)
5. Post!

**What Gets Shared:**
- Fabric image
- Name and category
- Pattern description
- #FabricFit hashtag

### Share Looks

**From Look Details:**
1. Open any saved look
2. Tap **share icon**
3. Choose platform
4. Share your creation!

### Export Wardrobe

**Create a PDF Catalog:**
1. Go to Profile → Settings
2. Scroll to "Export"
3. Tap "Export Wardrobe as PDF"
4. Choose what to include:
   - All fabrics
   - All looks
   - Specific collections
5. Tap "Export"
6. Share or save the PDF

**PDF Includes:**
- Cover page with your name
- Fabric images with details
- Look images with descriptions
- Professional layout

**Export Individual Items:**
- Long-press any fabric or look
- Tap "Export as Image"
- Get a branded image with details

## Accessibility Features

FabricFit is designed for everyone. Customize your experience:

### Finding Accessibility Settings

1. Go to **Profile** → **Settings**
2. Scroll to **Accessibility**
3. Adjust settings:

### Available Options

**High Contrast Mode:**
- Increases color contrast
- Makes text easier to read
- Better for low vision

**Reduced Motion:**
- Disables animations
- Reduces screen movement
- Helps with motion sensitivity

**Large Text:**
- Increases font sizes
- Makes content more readable
- Affects all text in app

**Haptic Feedback:**
- Vibration on interactions
- Confirms actions
- Can be turned off

### Language Support

FabricFit supports 4 languages:
- **English** (default)
- **Twi** (Akan)
- **Ga**
- **Ewe**

**To change language:**
1. Profile → Settings → Language
2. Select your preference
3. App restarts automatically

## Security & Privacy

### Biometric Lock

Protect your wardrobe with fingerprint or face unlock.

**Enable Biometric Lock:**
1. Profile → Settings → Security
2. Toggle "Biometric Lock"
3. Confirm with your fingerprint/face
4. Now required to open app

**Benefits:**
- Keeps your designs private
- Protects saved photos
- Prevents unauthorized access

**Disable:**
1. Open app with biometrics
2. Go to Settings → Security
3. Toggle off "Biometric Lock"

### Data Privacy

**Your Data:**
- All scans stored locally on your device
- Photos never uploaded without permission
- You control what gets shared
- Delete anytime

**Permissions We Use:**
- **Camera** - For scanning fabrics
- **Storage** - For saving images
- **Internet** - For AI features (optional)

**We Never:**
- Sell your data
- Share without permission
- Access photos without scanning
- Track your location

## FAQ

### General Questions

**Q: Is FabricFit free?**  
A: Yes! Core features are free. Premium features may be added later.

**Q: Does it work offline?**  
A: Most features work offline. AI features need internet for best results.

**Q: What devices are supported?**  
A: Android 7.0+ with camera. Works on phones and tablets.

**Q: How accurate is the AI?**  
A: 85-95% for common fabrics. Improves with better photos.

### Scanning Questions

**Q: Why didn't my scan work?**  
A: Check lighting, fabric position, and camera stability. Try again.

**Q: Can I scan any fabric?**  
A: Best with African fabrics (Kente, Ankara, etc.). Works with most patterns.

**Q: How many fabrics can I scan?**  
A: Unlimited! Scan as many as you want.

**Q: Where are scans stored?**  
A: On your device. Not uploaded unless you share.

### Design Questions

**Q: How does virtual try-on work?**  
A: AI analyzes your photo and fabric, generates realistic preview.

**Q: Why does my try-on look different?**  
A: Results vary by photo quality, pose, and lighting. Try different photos.

**Q: Can I use my own photos?**  
A: Yes! Upload any full-body photo for try-on.

**Q: How long does try-on take?**  
A: 10-30 seconds depending on complexity and connection.

### Sharing Questions

**Q: What gets shared?**  
A: Image + text with fabric details and #FabricFit hashtag.

**Q: Can I edit before sharing?**  
A: Yes! Edit caption in share dialog before posting.

**Q: Who can see my shared items?**  
A: Anyone on the platform you share to (Instagram, Twitter, etc.).

**Q: Can I share privately?**  
A: Yes! Use WhatsApp or messaging apps for private sharing.

### Privacy Questions

**Q: Is my data safe?**  
A: Yes! Stored locally, encrypted, never sold.

**Q: Can I delete my data?**  
A: Yes! Delete individual items or clear all data in Settings.

**Q: Do you track me?**  
A: Only basic analytics (no personal data). Can be disabled.

**Q: What if I lose my phone?**  
A: Your data is on the device. Export regularly for backup.

### Technical Questions

**Q: App is slow, what do I do?**  
A: Close other apps, restart FabricFit, check for updates.

**Q: Camera not working?**  
A: Grant camera permission in Settings → Apps → FabricFit.

**Q: Can't save scans?**  
A: Grant storage permission. Check available space.

**Q: How to update the app?**  
A: Google Play Store → My apps → FabricFit → Update.
